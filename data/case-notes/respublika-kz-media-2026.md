# Respublika.kz.media

> Updated after cross-checking against `Respublika_attack_waves_dataset.xlsx` and `AiPlex_MarkScan_investigation_summary.md` (project files). The campaign comprises 10 documented waves (W01–W10), not 6 as stated in the original version of this note.

## May 2025 — Telegram (W01)

In **May 2025**, Telegram removed 2 posts from the respublika.kz.media channel following a copyright complaint filed in the name of AiPlex Software Pvt Ltd. No public outcome of an appeal has been documented.

Confidence: Confirmed (AiPlex/MarkScan investigation summary, project file).

## January 2, 2026 — Facebook, editorial page (W02)

Respublika’s Facebook page (approximately 41,000 followers) was removed in its entirety after the outlet published an analysis of an AI-generated video of President Tokayev’s New Year’s address. The complainant was not disclosed to the editors. The page has not been restored.

Confidence: Confirmed (tirek.info, 2026-01-02; exclusive.kz).

## March 2026 — Facebook and Instagram, posts about the Constitution (W03, W04)

In **March 2026**, 57 posts about Kazakhstan’s Constitution were removed from Facebook, while all Instagram cards on the same subject were removed (the exact number was not specified). The stated basis was a trademark infringement complaint filed in the name of **Giorgio Armani** (a suspected fictitious complainant). The editors reported that the option to appeal was unavailable on either platform; the content has not been restored.

Confidence: Confirmed (exclusive.kz, 2026-03-07/08).

## March 2026 — Facebook, “AIPLEX” trademark (W05)

A separate complaint during the same period, filed in the name of AiPlex Software Pvt Ltd and alleging infringement of its own “AIPLEX” trademark, led to the removal of 6 Facebook posts. No public outcome has been documented.

Confidence: Confirmed (AiPlex/MarkScan investigation summary).

## March 2026 — Facebook/Instagram, parallel campaign against individuals (W08)

Also in March 2026, a separate wave targeted not the outlet’s account but the personal pages of journalist **Murat Daniyar** and lawyer **Murat Adam**, as well as Respublika.kz: all posts from 2026 were removed within approximately 10 minutes. The stated basis was the same “Giorgio Armani” scheme. No public outcome has been documented.

Confidence: Plausible (AiPlex/MarkScan investigation summary, single source).

## April 13–17, 2026 — Facebook, mass removal wave (W06)

Beginning on **April 13, 2026**, an automated wave of Facebook removals attributed to AiPlex Software Pvt Ltd escalated over several days, including the removal of the outlet’s profile logo.

**Source reconciliation:** A discrepancy had previously been identified between `Respublika_attack_waves_dataset.xlsx` (21 → 61 → 358) and `AiPlex_MarkScan_investigation_summary.md` (21 → 165 → 358). Following a team decision on September 25, 2026, **21 → 165 → 358** was accepted as the correct sequence of cumulative removals. The figure of 61 in the `.xlsx` dataset was deemed erroneous and needs to be corrected separately in the primary dataset. The attack remained active at the time of the last documented observation; the content had not been restored.

Confidence: Confirmed (respublikaKZmediaNEWS, April 2026; the figure of 165 was confirmed by the team on September 25, 2026).

## April 13, 2026 — Instagram, @alex_absolem (W07)

Another person targeted as part of the same campaign was Instagram blogger and activist **@alex_absolem**: more than 200 posts were removed following unfounded complaints attributed to AiPlex. Unlike in the other episodes, appeals were filed repeatedly, but removals continued while the appeals were still pending.

Confidence: Confirmed (Instagram appeal reel, DXIGTIvI1ot, 2026-04-13).

## December 2025–April 2026 — aggregate Instagram statistics (W09)

During this period, the editors recorded 7 separate instances of their Instagram page being blocked or removed and 3 instances of content being removed from the outlet’s Instagram account. The most recent page removal occurred on April 7, 2026. Some blocks were reversed; the content removed in the third instance had not been restored at the time of the report.

Confidence: Confirmed (respublikaKZmediaNEWS, “sad statistics,” April 2026).

## December 2025–April 2026 — editor-in-chief’s personal page (W10)

Separately from the outlet’s accounts, content was removed from editor-in-chief **Irina Petrushova’s** personal Facebook page 3 times during the same period following copyright complaints. No public outcome has been documented.

Confidence: Confirmed (respublikaKZmediaNEWS, April 2026).

---

**Overall classification:** Mechanism — predominantly fraudulent or bad-faith rights claims and mass automated submissions without verification; motive — political censorship by a state actor; relevant DSA articles — 17/20, 22 (AiPlex as a systematic high-volume submitter), 34/35. Meta does not publish data on individual submitters of DMCA or trademark complaints, so in most waves the complainant can be identified only through information available to those targeted.
