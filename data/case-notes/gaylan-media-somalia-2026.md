# Gaylan Media (Somalia)

## April 13, 2026

A member of the Federal Parliament of Somalia, **Dr. Abdillahi Hashi Abib**, issued a formal legal notice to **AiPlex Software Pvt. Ltd.**, **Meta Platforms Inc.**, and **Gaylan Media**, accusing them, in the notice’s words, of:

> “Fraudulent Takedown Claims, Abuse of Copyright Reporting Systems, and Coordinated Attempt to Suppress Protected Political Speech and Journalism”

Gaylan Media content was removed as part of the campaign (IDs #3095644373966135 and #841608584858218). The MP directly linked the campaign to the involvement of Somalia’s Deputy Minister of Information, **Abdirahman Al Kadaal**, and stated his intention to file lawsuits in U.S. courts.

## Classification (according to the project methodology)

- **Level 1 (mechanism):** A legitimate-looking but bad-faith copyright claim / mass automated submissions without verification.
- **Level 2 (target and motive):** Censorship alleged by a member of parliament (a formal accusation by a government body or parliamentarian); associated motive — suppression of protected political speech and journalism.
- **Level 3 (confidence):** Confirmed — documented by a public, formal legal notice issued by a sitting member of parliament. This is the first recorded case in the dataset of a formal accusation by a parliamentarian that AiPlex engaged in political censorship.
- **Level 4 (DSA provisions):** Articles 17/20 (notice-and-action); Article 22 (trusted flagger status requires establishment in the EU and is not directly applicable to AiPlex, but is relevant to analyzing privileged access to Meta); Articles 34/35 (systemic risks to freedom of expression).
- **Level 5 (jurisdiction/platform):** Affected party — Somalia; vendor — India (AiPlex); platform — Meta.
