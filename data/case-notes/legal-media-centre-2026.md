# Правовой медиа-центр (Legal Media Centre)

## February 2026 — YouTube

On **23 February 2026**, the public foundation's YouTube channel was blocked following copyright complaints concerning 13 videos. According to the foundation's SMM manager, quoted by Factcheck.kz, the complaints came from a user named "Najib Abdul Rahman Khalid"; whether this is a real person's name has not been established. The channel was restored the following day after a formal counter-notice.

## April 2026 — Instagram

In **April 2026**, the organization's Instagram account was fully deactivated following restrictions and copyright-related complaints reportedly associated with AiPlex Software Pvt Ltd, using an automated mass-filing pattern. No restoration has been recorded for this episode.

## 21 April 2026 — Facebook

Shortly after the Instagram takedown, the organization's Facebook page was blocked on **21 April 2026** under complaints of the same pattern as the Instagram episode (inferred AiPlex attribution, SRC-034). The page was restored after roughly a month following an editorial appeal.
