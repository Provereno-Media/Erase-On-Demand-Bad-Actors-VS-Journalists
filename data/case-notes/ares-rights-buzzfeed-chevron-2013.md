# Ares Rights — BuzzFeed & Chevron (Ecuador)

## 2013 — BuzzFeed

Documents concerning Ecuadorian government surveillance, hosted on cloud storage as part of a BuzzFeed investigation, were removed following a copyright complaint attributed to Ares Rights. The takedown was later reversed after a formal counter-notice was filed, and the documents were restored.

## 2013–2014 — Chevron

Videos on YouTube concerning the Chevron / Ecuador environmental litigation were removed following complaints associated with the same claimant. As with the BuzzFeed case, the material was restored after a formal counter-notice challenged the underlying copyright claim.

Motive category: political state censorship (BuzzFeed) and commercial reputation scrubbing (Chevron). Confidence: Confirmed.
