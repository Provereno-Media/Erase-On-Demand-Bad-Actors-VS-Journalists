# Eliminalia — OCCRP (CBH / Derwick Associates)

## 2019–2021

Between 2019 and 2021, investigators documented attempts to suppress OCCRP's reporting on CBH Compañía Barcelona de Hidrocarburos and Derwick Associates from Google Search results. The mechanism relied on a "backdated article" technique: a copy of OCCRP's article was published on a third-party site with an earlier displayed date, then used to claim that OCCRP had plagiarized the "original." Forensic attribution links the technique to Eliminalia.

Motive category: commercial reputation scrubbing. Confidence: Confirmed.
