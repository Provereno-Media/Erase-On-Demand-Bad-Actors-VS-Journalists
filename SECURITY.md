# Security & Data Protection Policy

The **Erase on Demand** project documents abuse of copyright and platform enforcement mechanisms against independent media and civil society. Because this research involves sensitive corporate entities, bad-faith actors, and vulnerable newsrooms, security and rigorous data protection are core priorities.

---

## 1. Responsible Disclosure & Reporting Vulnerabilities

If you discover a security vulnerability in our repository scripts, data pipelines, or automated infrastructure, please notify us directly rather than opening a public issue.

- **Email**: `security@provereno.media`
- **PGP Fingerprint**: Available on keyservers or upon request.
- **Response Timeline**: We acknowledge receipt of vulnerability reports within 48 hours and provide remediation updates within 7 days.

---

## 2. Personal Data & Privacy Protection (GDPR Compliance)

This project strictly adheres to European data protection standards (EU GDPR) and the mandate of the Tech Accountability Grants program (EFCSN / CERV):

### What is Published Publicly
- Corporate and organizational registry information (public company names, registration numbers, jurisdictions).
- Public statements, Lumen Database records, court filings, and verified journalistic reporting.
- Names of legal entities or public commercial vendors identified as filing takedown notices.

### What is Kept in the Closed Evidence Vault
- Non-public personal email addresses (e.g., private Gmail, ProtonMail, or internal personal accounts) used in notice submissions.
- Personal phone numbers, physical residential addresses, and government ID / passport scans.
- Unredacted email headers that could compromise confidential communication channels of impacted journalists.
- Raw correspondence with platform safety teams or intermediary support organizations where disclosure could jeopardize ongoing appeals.

---

## 3. Contributor & Source Safety

If you are a journalist, media manager, or researcher submitting evidence of an ongoing digital attack:
1. **Redact Sensitive Info**: Do not paste private contact details or unredacted communications in public GitHub issues or PRs.
2. **Use Secure Channels**: Request encrypted submission channels via `tech-accountability@provereno.media` if you face active legal threats, state surveillance, or physical intimidation.
3. **Attribution Control**: You may choose whether your contribution is credited publicly or anonymized in commit logs and source attributions.
