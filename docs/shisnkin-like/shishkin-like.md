# Shishkin Like

## 2023

The first blocking of the Shishkin Like project's account occurred in **March 2023**: there were several complaints, allegedly from Kazakhstani companies and educational institutions (all of them were filed from fake accounts), about supposed copyright infringement (publishing company logos in illustrations for reviews of various market segments).

The editorial team collected letters from these very companies and organizations stating that they had not filed any complaints, and sent them to Meta. However, not a single real person ever got in touch (even with the involvement of the Internews network) — and the bot ultimately blocked and then completely deleted the editorial team's account.

## 2026

The second case occurred in **April 2026**: the account was first blocked for allegedly infringing third-party copyrights, and then completely deleted for allegedly "sexual harassment of adults."

This time the editorial team reached out to the organization **Access Now**, and within three weeks the account was restored.
