About this research

The central object of analysis is not individual vendors but the structural failure of platform enforcement systems: privileged "trusted" reporting channels, automated takedown pipelines that operate without content verification, and the absence of effective oversight under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA). The project is developed by Provereno Media under the EU Citizens, Equality, Rights and Values (CERV) programme through the European Fact-Checking Standards Network (EFCSN).

About the data

Each case is a coded platform-level episode. Related episodes may share a campaign ID. Confidence describes the evidence level assigned to a case; claimant naming and vendor attribution should be read separately from the established platform action.

Confirmed, Unverified and Plausible are dataset classifications, not judicial findings. For inclusion rules, definitions and limitations, consult the methodology and codebook.
